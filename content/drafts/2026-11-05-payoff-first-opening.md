## 1) Telegram channel — text-only version

🎯 CREATOR TIP: Show the payoff before the intro

If your video opens with a long greeting, viewers still don't know why they should stay.

Try this 3-step opening:

1. **Show the result first** — the best moment, finished result, or a clear question.
2. **Give one line of context** — explain what the viewer is about to see.
3. **Deliver the promise quickly** — don't make the opening more exciting than the actual video.

For a gameplay Short, open with the goal, save, or skill move. For a tutorial, show the finished result before the steps.

After publishing, compare the first 5–10 seconds of retention across your next three uploads. Change one thing at a time; treat it as an experiment, not a guaranteed growth trick.
