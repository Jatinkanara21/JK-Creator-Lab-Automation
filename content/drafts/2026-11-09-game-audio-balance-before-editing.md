## 1) Telegram — text-only creator tip

🎧 CREATOR TIP: Check your audio before editing

A gameplay clip can look sharp and still be difficult to watch if the important sound is buried.

Before recording a full session:

1. Record a 10-second test with both game audio and your microphone.
2. Listen through headphones at an ordinary phone volume.
3. Make commentary easy to hear without letting it peak or distort.
4. Check a loud moment and a quiet moment—not only the menu screen.
5. Record one more short test after adjusting the levels.

Do the sound check before you spend time editing. Clear audio helps viewers follow the action and understand your callouts.
