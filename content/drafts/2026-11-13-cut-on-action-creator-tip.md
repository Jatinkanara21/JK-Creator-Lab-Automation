## Telegram channel post

🎮 **EDITING TIP: CUT ON ACTION**

Your edit may not need more transitions. It may need better cuts.

Try this:
1. Find a clear movement — a sprint, jump, shot, pass or card reveal.
2. Cut just before that movement finishes.
3. Start the next shot while the action is still moving.
4. Align a sound effect with the visible impact only when it helps the moment.
5. Watch once with the sound off. If the action still feels clear, the cut is doing its job.

Use fewer transitions; let the movement carry the edit.
