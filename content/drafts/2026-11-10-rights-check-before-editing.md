## 1) Telegram — text-only creator tip

🧾 CREATOR TIP: Keep an asset-rights log

Before you edit, list every asset that did not originate in your project:

1. Music and sound effects
2. Images, clips and overlays
3. Fonts, templates and graphics
4. Gameplay footage or third-party recordings

For each one, record the source, permission or license, allowed platforms, commercial-use limits, attribution requirements and any expiry date.

A sound that is available inside one app is not automatically cleared for every exported video or platform. If the rights are unclear, replace it with original or properly cleared material.

Keep the proof with the project file—not only in your memory.
