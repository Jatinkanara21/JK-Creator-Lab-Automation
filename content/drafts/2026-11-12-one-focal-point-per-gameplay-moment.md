## 1) Telegram — text-only post

🎮 CREATOR TIP: Give every gameplay moment one focal point

When a clip shows the player, score, kill feed, subtitles, arrows and zoom effects all at once, viewers may not know where to look.

Before exporting:
1. Decide what the viewer should notice first.
2. Keep the main action visible; avoid covering it with captions.
3. Use one callout only when it explains something important.
4. Let the score or result appear long enough to read.
5. Remove effects that do not help the moment.

A clean frame makes the action easier to understand on a small phone screen.

A gameplay edit can become difficult to follow when every effect competes for attention. Choose the one thing viewers should notice first, keep the action clear, and use captions or zooms only when they add context. A clean frame often communicates more than a crowded one.
