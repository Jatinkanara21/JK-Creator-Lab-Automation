# Creator Tip — Caption in Thought Groups, Not Word-by-Word Noise

**Date prepared:** 14 November 2026  
**Status:** DRAFT — not approved, not queued, not scheduled, not published  
**Purpose:** Original educational content for JK Creator Lab's Telegram channel, Instagram account, and the connected J.K. GAMER 21 YouTube channel.

## Core lesson

Captions should make a video easier to follow, not compete with the action. Break speech into short meaning-based phrases, change the caption when the thought changes, and keep the text away from important visual information.

## Practical editing checklist

1. Split captions at natural phrase boundaries instead of showing a full sentence at once.
2. Keep each caption short enough to read comfortably on a phone; remove filler words when they do not change the meaning.
3. Use consistent font weight, strong contrast and a subtle background or shadow when the footage is busy.
4. Keep captions clear of the game HUD, score, kill feed, player names, important action and faces.
5. For anime edits, do not cover existing dialogue subtitles or on-screen signs; confirm that any added translation is accurate.
6. Preview the export on a small phone screen with sound off. Check spelling, timing and whether every caption can be read before it disappears.
7. Do not stack English and Hindi captions into a tiny block. Choose the intended language for that edit or make separate language versions.
