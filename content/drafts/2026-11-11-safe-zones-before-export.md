## 1) Telegram — text-only creator tip

📱 CREATOR TIP: Check your vertical-video safe zones

A caption can be perfectly written and still become unreadable if an app button covers it.

Before exporting a Short or Reel:

1. Keep the main caption away from the very top and bottom edges.
2. Leave space on the right for platform controls.
3. Do not place scores, player names or important gameplay action under interface overlays.
4. For anime edits, keep character faces and subtitles clear of the same areas.
5. Preview the finished edit on a phone in the target app before publishing.

Different platforms place controls differently. Recheck the safe areas for each destination instead of exporting one layout blindly.
