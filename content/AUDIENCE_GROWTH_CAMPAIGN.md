# JK Creator Lab — 7-day audience growth campaign

Audience: small and growing YouTube creators who need practical help with titles, descriptions, thumbnails, hooks and upload planning.

## Seven daily post ideas

**Day 1 — Titles:** A strong title accurately tells viewers what they will get. Name the actual subject, add a specific outcome or moment, remove vague filler, and ensure it matches the video. Ask: comment TITLE for a checklist.

**Day 2 — Descriptions:** Make the first lines explain the video naturally. Put the main topic early, tell viewers what is inside, and avoid keyword stuffing. Ask: comment DESCRIPTION for a structure.

**Day 3 — Tags:** Tags are not a magic ranking switch. Prioritize an accurate title, useful thumbnail and a video that delivers what it promises. Ask: titles, thumbnails or retention — what is hardest?

**Day 4 — Thumbnails:** Communicate one idea at phone-screen size. Use a clear subject, readable contrast and few words. Never promise a scene that is not in the video. Ask: comment THUMBNAIL with your biggest challenge.

**Day 5 — Hooks:** Start with the payoff, question or most interesting moment. Remove long greetings and unrelated intros. Make the opening match the title and thumbnail. Ask: what is your next video's opening hook?

**Day 6 — Checklist:** Check title accuracy, preview thumbnail at small size, make the first description lines useful, check audio and captions, add chapters only when useful, and verify links.
