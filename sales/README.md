# Sales operations (manual, free)

- `leads.csv`: record enquiries without collecting unnecessary personal data.
- `orders.csv`: record order ID, product/service, agreed price, payment status and delivery status.
- `revenue.csv`: record only verified receipts, refunds and fees.
- Never mark an order paid based only on a screenshot. Verify the UPI credit in your own bank/UPI app.
- Never store UPI PINs, OTPs, bank passwords, full card details, or customers' sensitive documents here.
- Do not publish a personal UPI ID or payment QR until the owner adds and verifies it.
- Keep customer details private; do not commit names, phone numbers, UPI IDs or addresses to this public repository. Use anonymous order references only.
- Refunds, delivery and revision terms should be shown before payment.
